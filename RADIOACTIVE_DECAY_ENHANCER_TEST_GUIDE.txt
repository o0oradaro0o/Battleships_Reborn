===============================================================================
RADIOACTIVE DECAY ENHANCER - TESTING GUIDE
Quick Reference for In-Game Testing
===============================================================================

ITEM NAME: Radioactive Decay Enhancer
ITEM CODE: item_poison_light_mix_ult_bow
COST: 13,500 gold

===============================================================================
QUICK TEST PROCEDURE
===============================================================================

1. SPAWN THE WEAPON
   Console command:
   -item item_poison_light_mix_ult_bow

2. BASIC FIRING TEST
   ✓ Stand near enemies
   ✓ Weapon should auto-fire every 0.3 seconds
   ✓ Watch for green-yellow projectiles
   ✓ Listen for "Hero_Alchemist.UnstableConcoction.Fuse" sound

3. VERIFY DEBUFF APPLICATION
   ✓ Hit an enemy 3-5 times
   ✓ Check enemy's status bar for Radioactive Decay debuff icon
   ✓ Debuff should show stack count (1-10)
   ✓ Each hit should increment stack count

4. TEST DAMAGE AMPLIFICATION
   ✓ Apply 5 stacks to an enemy
   ✓ Equip a different weapon
   ✓ Hit the enemy with the other weapon
   ✓ Observe damage numbers:
     - Base damage from weapon
     - +125 bonus damage from decay (5 stacks × 25)
   ✓ Listen for "Hero_Venomancer.PoisonNova" sound on decay proc
   ✓ Watch for green spark particles

5. VERIFY STACK CAP
   ✓ Hit enemy 15+ times
   ✓ Stacks should cap at 10
   ✓ Further hits refresh duration but don't add stacks

6. TEST DURATION
   ✓ Apply stacks to enemy
   ✓ Stop attacking for 6+ seconds
   ✓ Debuff should expire after 5 seconds

===============================================================================
DETAILED TEST SCENARIOS
===============================================================================

TEST 1: SINGLE TARGET, SINGLE WEAPON
Purpose: Verify basic weapon functionality

Steps:
1. Spawn a practice target (enemy hero/creep)
2. Equip Radioactive Decay Enhancer only
3. Attack target for 10 seconds
4. Observe:
   - Projectile firing rate (should be consistent 0.3s)
   - Stack accumulation (should reach 10 stacks quickly)
   - Base damage (125 per hit)
   - Debuff visual effects (green aura, ground effect, glow)

Expected Result:
- Target gains 10 stacks of Radioactive Decay
- Each hit after 10 stacks deals 125 + (10 × 25) = 375 total damage
- Debuff remains active as long as hits continue

---

TEST 2: MULTI-WEAPON SYNERGY
Purpose: Verify damage amplification from other weapons

Steps:
1. Spawn a practice target with high HP (e.g., enemy hero)
2. Equip Radioactive Decay Enhancer + another weapon
3. Let Radioactive Decay apply 10 stacks
4. Switch focus to other weapon
5. Compare damage with and without decay stacks

Expected Result:
- Other weapon's damage is amplified by +250 per hit (10 stacks)
- Green sparks appear on each amplified hit
- "Hero_Venomancer.PoisonNova" sound plays on each proc

Example with Ice Cannon (50 dmg):
- Without decay: 50 damage per hit
- With 10 decay stacks: 50 + 250 = 300 damage per hit

---

TEST 3: RAPID FIRE SYNERGY
Purpose: Test with high fire-rate weapons

Steps:
1. Equip Radioactive Decay Enhancer + rapid-fire weapon (e.g., Light weapon)
2. Attack single target
3. Monitor damage output over time

Expected Result:
- Decay stacks applied quickly (10 stacks in ~3 seconds)
- Rapid-fire weapon gains +250 damage per shot immediately
- Massive DPS increase observable
- Visual/audio feedback becomes intense (many procs)

---

TEST 4: AOE WEAPON SYNERGY
Purpose: Verify decay works with area damage

Steps:
1. Spawn multiple enemy targets in a group
2. Apply decay stacks to all targets (5 stacks each)
3. Use AOE weapon (e.g., Breach weapon, Chaos weapon)
4. Observe damage numbers

Expected Result:
- Each enemy in AOE takes base damage + (5 × 25) = +125 bonus
- All enemies show decay proc effects simultaneously
- Effective area damage significantly increased

---

TEST 5: STACK DECAY (DURATION TEST)
Purpose: Verify 5-second duration and refresh mechanics

Steps:
1. Apply 5 stacks to an enemy
2. Wait 3 seconds (don't attack)
3. Apply 1 more stack
4. Wait 5 more seconds

Expected Result:
- After step 2: Stacks still at 5 (duration < 5s)
- After step 3: Stacks at 6, duration refreshed to 5s
- After step 4: All stacks expired, debuff removed

---

TEST 6: MULTIPLE DAMAGE SOURCES
Purpose: Verify decay triggers on ALL damage types

Steps:
1. Apply 10 decay stacks to enemy
2. Test damage from:
   - Physical damage weapon
   - Magical damage weapon
   - Pure damage weapon
   - Environmental damage (if available)

Expected Result:
- ALL damage types trigger the +250 bonus
- No exceptions or failures

---

TEST 7: INFINITE LOOP PREVENTION
Purpose: Verify decay damage doesn't trigger itself

Steps:
1. Apply 10 decay stacks to enemy
2. Hit enemy once with any weapon
3. Observe damage instances

Expected Result:
- First damage: Base weapon damage
- Second damage: +250 decay bonus
- No third damage instance (decay doesn't trigger itself)
- No cascade/chain reaction

---

TEST 8: BUILDING TARGET TEST
Purpose: Verify weapon works on buildings

Steps:
1. Target enemy tower/building
2. Apply decay stacks
3. Hit with other weapons

Expected Result:
- Decay applies and stacks normally
- Buildings take amplified damage
- All visual effects appear correctly

---

TEST 9: PURGE TEST
Purpose: Verify debuff can be purged

Steps:
1. Apply 10 decay stacks to enemy
2. Use purge effect (if available in mod)

Expected Result:
- Debuff removed immediately
- Visual effects disappear
- No more bonus damage procs

---

TEST 10: DEATH & RESPAWN
Purpose: Handle edge cases

Steps:
1. Apply decay stacks to enemy
2. Kill enemy
3. Enemy respawns

Expected Result:
- Debuff removed on death
- No lingering effects
- Fresh enemy has no stacks

===============================================================================
COMMON ISSUES & SOLUTIONS
===============================================================================

ISSUE: Weapon doesn't fire
Solution: Check that weapon_passive ability exists on caster

ISSUE: Debuff doesn't apply
Solution: Verify target is valid enemy unit (not invulnerable)

ISSUE: Damage amplification too high/low
Solution: Check decay_damage_per_stack value (should be 25)

ISSUE: Infinite loop / game freeze
Solution: Verify DOTA_DAMAGE_FLAG_REFLECTION check is working

ISSUE: Stacks don't refresh
Solution: Check SetDuration(decay_duration, true) call

ISSUE: Visual effects missing
Solution: Verify particle paths are correct and files exist
