===========================================
UNIQUE MECHANIC: MAGMA TRAIL
===========================================

HOW IT WORKS:
1. When projectile hits a target:
   - Deals 860 damage to primary target
   - Applies "Magma Trail" debuff (7 seconds)
   - Creates impact explosion effect

2. While debuffed, target leaves magma trail:
   - Creates trail points every 0.2 seconds
   - Each trail point lasts 4.5 seconds
   - Trail points use CreateModifierThinker() with aura
   - Visual: Phoenix fire spirit ground effects

3. Units in magma trails:
   - Take 70 damage every 0.5 seconds
   - Slowed by 25%
   - Multiple trails can affect same unit
   - Creates area denial zones

STRATEGIC VALUE:
✓ Area denial (enemies must move or take damage)
✓ Zone control (trails block paths)
✓ Punishes slow/stationary targets
✓ Synergy with other CC abilities
✓ Visual feedback of danger zones

COUNTERPLAY:
✓ Enemies can move out of trails
✓ Trails have limited duration
✓ Fast-moving targets leave shorter trails
✓ Spread out positioning reduces trail overlap

PARTICLE EFFECTS USED:

   - particles/basic_projectile/magma_trail_cannon_projectile.vpcf (custom)

4. Ground Trail:
   - particles/units/heroes/hero_phoenix/phoenix_fire_spirit_ground.vpcf
   
5. Trail Damage (on units in trail):
   - particles/units/heroes/hero_phoenix/phoenix_icarus_dive_burn.vpcf

SOUND EFFECTS USED:
- Hero_Phoenix.LaunchFire (projectile fire)
- Hero_Lina.DragonSlave.Cast (impact)
- Hero_Phoenix.FireSpirits.Launch (trail debuff applied)
- Hero_Phoenix.FireSpirits.Target (trail damage ticks)

MODIFIER STRUCTURE:
```
modifier_item_fire_coal_mix_ult_bow (main weapon)
  ├── OnIntervalThink(): Fire 2 projectiles per volley
  └── Calls OnProjectileHit() when projectile hits

modifier_magma_trail_debuff (on hit target)
  ├── Duration: 7 seconds
  ├── OnIntervalThink (0.2s): Create trail points
  ├── DeclareFunctions(): Apply -15% slow to carrier
  └── Visual: Ember Spirit searing chains effect

modifier_magma_trail_thinker_aura (trail point)
  ├── Duration: 4.5 seconds
  ├── IsAura(): true (emits aura to enemies)
  ├── Radius: 225
  └── Visual: Phoenix fire spirit ground effect

modifier_magma_trail_aura_debuff (on units in trail)
  ├── OnIntervalThink (0.5s): Deal 70 damage
  ├── DeclareFunctions(): Apply -25% slow
  └── Visual: Phoenix icarus dive burn effect
```

PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATIONS:
- Uses CreateModifierThinker() for trail points (efficient)
- Particles automatically cleaned up on thinker expiration
- All particles precached to prevent first-hit lag
- Aura system handles unit tracking efficiently
- No manual unit tracking or loops needed
