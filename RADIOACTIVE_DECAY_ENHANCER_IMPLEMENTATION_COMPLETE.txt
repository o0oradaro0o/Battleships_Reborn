HOW THE WEAPON WORKS
===============================================================================

PRIMARY WEAPON BEHAVIOR:
- Fires radioactive projectiles at enemies every 0.3 seconds
- Base damage: 125 physical damage per hit
- Range: 650 units
- Projectile speed: 1200
- Each hit applies 1 stack of Radioactive Decay (max 10 stacks)

RADIOACTIVE DECAY DEBUFF MECHANIC:
- Each stack lasts 5 seconds (refreshes independently)
- For EVERY instance of damage the affected unit takes from ANY source:
  * Calculate: bonus_damage = stack_count × 25
  * Apply bonus_damage as magical damage
  * This damage does NOT trigger itself (prevents infinite loop)

EXAMPLE SYNERGY:
- Enemy has 5 stacks of Radioactive Decay
- Another weapon hits for 100 damage
- Radioactive Decay triggers: 5 × 25 = 125 bonus damage
- Total damage dealt: 100 + 125 = 225 damage

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
===============================================================================

KEY MODIFIERS:

1. modifier_item_poison_light_mix_ult_bow (wielder modifier)
   - Hidden passive modifier on player
   - Handles weapon firing logic via OnIntervalThink
   - Fires tracking projectiles every 0.3 seconds
   - Finds random enemy target in 650 unit range

2. modifier_radioactive_decay_debuff (enemy debuff)
   - Visible stacking debuff (1-10 stacks)
   - Duration: 5 seconds per stack
   - Implements MODIFIER_EVENT_ON_TAKEDAMAGE
   - Calculates and applies bonus damage on any damage instance
   - Uses DOTA_DAMAGE_FLAG_REFLECTION to prevent infinite loop

DAMAGE EVENT HANDLING:
- OnTakeDamage() checks if params.unit == debuff parent
- Verifies damage is not from Radioactive Decay itself
- Checks for DOTA_DAMAGE_FLAG_REFLECTION flag to prevent recursion
- Applies bonus magical damage with NO_SPELL_AMPLIFICATION flag

PROJECTILE SYSTEM:
- Uses ProjectileManager:CreateTrackingProjectile()
- Ability:OnProjectileHit() handles impact logic
- Deals base damage and applies/refreshes decay stacks
- Shows impact particle and sound effects

- Creates sustained pressure on affected targets
