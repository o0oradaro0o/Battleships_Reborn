WEAPON OVERVIEW:
----------------
The Radioactive Decay Enhancer is a poison/light hybrid ultimate weapon that applies stacking "Radioactive Decay" debuffs to enemies. The key mechanic is that units affected by Radioactive Decay take ADDITIONAL FLAT DAMAGE from ALL damage sources while the debuff is active. This creates a multiplicative damage amplification effect that synergizes with all other weapons.

CORE MECHANICS:
---------------
1. PRIMARY WEAPON BEHAVIOR:
   - Fires tracking projectiles at enemies (similar to other ultimate weapons)
   - Fire rate: 0.3 seconds
   - Base damage: 125 physical damage
   - Range: 650 units
   - Projectile speed: 1200
   - Each projectile applies 1 stack of Radioactive Decay on hit

2. RADIOACTIVE DECAY DEBUFF:
   - Stacks: 1-10 stacks (applied one per hit)
   - Duration: 5 seconds per stack (refreshes independently)
   - Visual: Green-yellow glowing corrosive aura on affected unit
   
   DAMAGE AMPLIFICATION MECHANIC:
   - For each stack of Radioactive Decay, the affected unit takes +25 flat damage 
     whenever they take damage from ANY source
   - Example: Enemy has 3 stacks of Radioactive Decay
     * They take 100 damage from another weapon
     * Radioactive Decay triggers: 3 stacks × 25 = 75 additional damage
     * Total damage dealt: 100 + 75 = 175 damage
   
   - This additional damage should be:
     * MAGICAL damage type
     * Triggered on ANY damage instance (physical, magical, pure)
     * Does NOT stack with itself (Radioactive Decay damage won't trigger more Decay damage)
     * Applies to damage from: other weapons, abilities, environmental damage, etc.

3. VISUAL DESIGN:
   - Weapon projectile should have a radioactive/toxic appearance
   - Color scheme: Sickly green-yellow with hints of bright light/energy
   - Particle effects to consider:
     * Projectile: Green-yellow energy with corrosive drips/sparks
     * Trail: Toxic vapor trail with glowing particles
     * Impact: Small corrosive explosion with radiation burst
     * Debuff effect: Glowing green aura with decay particles around unit

---------------------------
1. DAMAGE EVENT HANDLING:
   - Use MODIFIER_EVENT_ON_TAKEDAMAGE in DeclareFunctions
   - In OnTakeDamage(params):
     * params.unit = unit taking damage
     * params.attacker = unit dealing damage
     * params.damage = actual damage dealt
     * params.damage_type = type of damage
   
2. PREVENT INFINITE LOOP:
   - When applying bonus decay damage, mark it somehow to prevent triggering itself
   - Option 1: Use params.inflictor to check if damage source is this ability
   - Option 2: Add a flag to the damage table: damage_flags with DOTA_DAMAGE_FLAG_NO_SPELL_AMPLIFICATION

3. STACK MANAGEMENT:
   - Each hit adds 1 stack (up to 10 max)
   - Each stack has independent 5-second duration
   - Use :SetStackCount() and :GetStackCount()
   - On refresh, increment stacks but don't reset all durations

4. PERFORMANCE CONSIDERATION:
   - OnTakeDamage fires for EVERY damage instance
   - Keep logic lightweight
   - Cache values (stack count, damage per stack) rather than recalculating

-----------------------

- Stack cap of 10 prevents infinite scaling (max +250 damage per hit)
- 5-second duration means stacks naturally decay in prolonged fights
- Weapon excels at sustained focus fire on single targets
- Less effective against rapidly spawning enemies (stacks don't transfer)
