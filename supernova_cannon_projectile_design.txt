===================================================================
SUPERNOVA CANNON PROJECTILE DESIGN
===================================================================

FILE: supernova_cannon_projectile.vpcf
LOCATION: content/battleships_traders/particles/basic_projectile/

DESIGN CONCEPT:
---------------
The Supernova Cannon projectile combines the visual elements of a miniature sun 
with plasma energy trails, creating a devastating solar-themed missile effect 
that matches the weapon's explosive power and theme.

VISUAL ELEMENTS:
----------------
The projectile is composed of 8 layered trail particle effects from Dota 2 heroes.
All effects are trail-based to ensure they fade properly when the projectile hits:

1. **Jakiro Base Attack Fire Trail** (Primary Fire Layer)
   - Creates the core fiery trail effect
   - Provides the primary orange/yellow fire streaks

2. **Invoker Chaos Meteor Fire Trail B** (Meteor Fire)
   - Adds the meteor-like burning trail
   - Intense orange/red fire effect that streams behind

3. **Lina Laguna Blade Sparks Trail** (Electric Energy)
   - Adds crackling lightning/energy sparks
   - Creates the bright white-yellow electrical energy

4. **Ember Spirit Remnant Dash Embers** (Floating Embers)
   - Adds floating fire ember particles trailing behind
   - Creates depth and particle dispersion

5. **Abaddon Death Coil Alliance Trail B** (Plasma Energy)
   - Adds the purple/blue plasma energy trails
   - Combines the "plasma" element with the "fire" element

6. **Phoenix Base Attack Trail** (Solar Fire)
   - Phoenix's core attack trail for authentic solar theme
   - Bright fiery trail that screams "sun magic"

7. **Batrider Flamebreak Trail 3** (Intense Fire Trail)
   - Heavy fire trail effect for visual weight
   - Deep orange flames that add density

8. **Necronomicon Archer Projectile Launch** (Magic Glow)
   - Magical energy glow at the projectile core
   - Adds mystical element to the weapon

COLOR SCHEME:
-------------
- Primary: Bright orange/yellow (solar fire)
- Secondary: White/bright yellow (intense heat/energy)
- Accent: Purple/blue plasma trails
- Overall: Looks like a miniature sun or supernova traveling through space

SIZE & SCALE:
-------------
- Constant Radius: 64.0 (larger than standard projectiles at 48.0)
- This makes the projectile more imposing and befitting an ultimate weapon

THEMATIC FIT:
-------------
The projectile perfectly captures the "Supernova Cannon" theme:
- Solar/sun imagery (Phoenix, Invoker sun strike)
- Explosive fire energy (Lina, Batrider, Ember)
- Plasma/energy trails (Abaddon's purple coils)
- Meteor/cosmic element (Chaos Meteor)
- Ultimate weapon scale and impact

TECHNICAL NOTES:
----------------
- Uses standard projectile framework (velocity, tracking, etc.)
- Compatible with existing weapon systems
- All particle effects are from official Dota 2 heroes
- No custom particles needed - all references are to existing game files

VISUAL IMPACT:
--------------
When fired, the projectile will appear as a blazing comet of fire and plasma
energy, with multiple layered trails creating a thick, intense stream of flame
and magical energy. The combination of fire trails (orange/red), plasma energy
(purple/blue), and electric sparks (white/yellow) creates a unique visual
signature that immediately identifies this as the Supernova Cannon.

This creates a cohesive visual story:
1. Launch: Explosive burst of fire and plasma energy
2. Flight: Thick, multi-layered trail of fire, embers, plasma, and sparks
3. Impact: Supernova explosion (Invoker sun strike particle)
4. Result: Enemies pushed away by the physics-based shockwave

KEY IMPROVEMENTS OVER ORIGINAL:
- All trail-based effects ensure proper fade on impact
- No hero models or ground effects that would linger
- Combines fire (Phoenix, Jakiro, Batrider) + plasma (Abaddon) + energy (Lina)
- Thick, visible projectile appropriate for an ultimate weapon
- Clear visual distinction from other weapons in the game
