CRITICAL IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
================================================================================

1. ✅ ICE WEAPON TARGETING (CORRECTED):
   - Ice weapons use: DOTA_UNIT_TARGET_HERO + DOTA_UNIT_TARGET_BASIC
   - Does NOT include DOTA_UNIT_TARGET_BUILDING
   - This is the CORRECTED behavior (previously was hitting towers)

2. ✅ WIND WEAPON TARGETING:
   - Wind weapons use: DOTA_UNIT_TARGET_HERO + DOTA_UNIT_TARGET_BASIC + DOTA_UNIT_TARGET_BUILDING
   - CAN hit towers and structures

3. ✅ DUAL MODIFIER SYSTEM:
   - Primary modifier (ice) adds secondary modifier (wind) in OnCreated
   - Primary modifier removes secondary modifier in OnDestroy
   - Both weapons fire independently at their own fire rates

4. ✅ PROJECTILE SYSTEM:
   - Uses weapon_passive ability for projectile firing
   - Each weapon maintains its own projectile count (1 for levels 1-3, 4 for ultimate)
   - Ice particles: ice_one/two/three/ult_projectile.vpcf
   - Wind particles: wind_one/two/three/ult_projectile.vpcf

5. ✅ WIND WEAPON MECHANICS:
   - Distance-based damage (reduced damage inside minimum range)
   - Knockback effect (excluded for buildings)
   - Ally healing (400 radius, excludes self)
   - Minimum range: 600 units
   - Minimum range damage: 50%

6. ✅ ICE WEAPON MECHANICS:
   - Movement speed slow (negative percentage)
   - Slow duration varies by level
   - Visual debuff: ice_debuff.vpcf (levels 1-3), ice_ult_slow.vpcf (ultimate)
