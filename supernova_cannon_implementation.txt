SUPERNOVA CANNON - PLASMA/FIRE MIX ULTIMATE WEAPON
===================================================================

WEAPON NAME: Supernova Cannon
ITEM CODE: item_plasma_fire_mix_ult_bow

CONCEPT:
--------
The Supernova Cannon is a unique ultimate weapon that replaces the previous dual-weapon system (plasma gun + fire gun) with a single devastating cannon that creates supernova explosions on impact.

UNIQUE MECHANICS:
-----------------
1. DUAL PROJECTILES: Fires 2 projectiles per volley, each dealing 860 damage (1720 total per target if both hit)
2. PRIMARY DAMAGE: Each projectile deals 860 damage to the direct target hit
3. SUPERNOVA EXPLOSION: Creates a blast radius of 500 units around the impact point for each projectile
4. PHYSICS-BASED KNOCKBACK: Pushes all enemies (except the primary target) away from the impact using smooth physics simulation (like Force Staff)
5. DIMINISHING PUSH: The knockback velocity scales inversely with the number of units affected
   - Formula: push_velocity = base_push_distance / sqrt(num_enemies)
   - Example: 1 enemy = 200 velocity, 4 enemies = 100 velocity, 9 enemies = 67 velocity
6. BUILDINGS: Buildings are not pushed back (only non-building units)
7. SMOOTH MOVEMENT: Units are pushed with physics velocity over 0.5 seconds, not instant teleportation

WEAPON STATS:
-------------
- Base Damage: 860 (per projectile)
- Projectiles per Volley: 2
- Total Damage per Volley: 1720
- Fire Rate: 3.5 seconds
- Range: 600
- Projectile Speed: 1450
- DPS: 491 (860 × 2 / 3.5)
- Push Radius: 500 units
- Max Push Distance: 200 units (diminishes with more units)

9. ADDED: Area denial through displacement

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION:
-------------------------
The knockback system uses the game's Physics:Unit framework:
- Physics:Unit(enemy) initializes physics simulation on the target
- SetPhysicsVelocity(vector) applies initial push velocity
- SetPhysicsFriction(0.1) creates smooth deceleration
- StartPhysicsSimulation() begins the movement
- Timer stops physics after 0.5 seconds
- This creates smooth, arc-like movement instead of instant displacement

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY:
------------------
The Supernova Cannon focuses on:
- Burst damage to a single target
- Tactical displacement of enemy groups
- Risk/reward: More enemies = less effective displacement
- Zone control through forced repositioning
- Matches power scale of other ultimate weapons (similar DPS to Atom Splitter)

BALANCE CONSIDERATIONS:
-----------------------
- DPS of 491 is moderate for an ultimate weapon (balanced, not overpowered)
- Fires 2 projectiles per volley for reliability against moving targets
- Knockback provides utility without excessive displacement (200 base distance)
- Diminishing returns prevent it from being OP against large groups
- Buildings immune to knockback prevents exploit against structures
- 3.5s fire rate creates meaningful windows of vulnerability
- Total burst damage of 1720 per target (if both projectiles hit) provides good kill potential

TACTICAL USAGE:
---------------
- Best against clustered enemies (creates space and splits groups)
- Strong for peeling enemies off allies
- Can disrupt enemy formations and positioning
- Less effective against spread-out targets
- Strategic for controlling choke points and objectives
