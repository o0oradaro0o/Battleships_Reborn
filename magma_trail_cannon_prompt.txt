NEW WEAPON DESIGN: MAGMA TRAIL CANNON

THEME:
A devastating magma cannon that leaves burning trails of lava wherever it hits. Combines fire's burning intensity with coal's lingering area denial.

WEAPON STATS (Target ~490 DPS):

- Fire Rate: 3.5 seconds
- Range: 650

- Projectiles per Volley: 2
- Target DPS: 491
- Item Cost: 13,500

UNIQUE MECHANIC: MAGMA TRAIL

When a projectile hits a target:
1. Deal direct damage to the primary target (860)
2. Apply "Magma Trail" debuff to the target (duration: 6-8 seconds)
3. Target leaves a magma trail as it moves
4. Any unit that enters or remains in the trail takes damage and is slowed

MAGMA TRAIL PARAMETERS:
- Trail Duration on Ground: 4-5 seconds after being created
- Trail Creation Rate: Create trail point every 0.2 seconds while debuff active
- Trail Damage Tick Rate: 0.5 seconds
- Trail Damage per Tick: 60-80 (adjust for balance)
- Trail Radius: 200-250
- Slow Amount: 20-30%
- Trail Visual: Ground fire/lava effects

IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH:

1. OnProjectileHit():
   - Deal primary damage to target
   - Apply modifier_magma_trail_debuff to target
   - Create impact explosion particles

2. modifier_magma_trail_debuff (on hit unit):
   - IsDebuff: true
   - Duration: 6-8 seconds

   - Store trail point data (location, creation time)

   - Trail points should visually connect or overlap slightly

===========================================
BALANCE CONSIDERATIONS
===========================================

DPS CALCULATION:
- Direct Hit DPS: (860 × 2) / 3.5 = 491 DPS
- Trail DPS: 70 damage per 0.5s tick = 140 DPS (if unit stays in trail)
- Total Potential DPS: 491 + 140 = 631 DPS (very strong but requires target to stay in trail)

The trail provides:
1. Area denial (enemies must move or take extra damage)
2. Zone control (trails can block paths)
3. Punishes slow/stationary targets
4. Synergy with other CC abilities

Counterplay:
- Enemies can move out of trails
- Trails have limited duration
- Fast-moving targets leave shorter trails
- Spread out positioning reduces trail overlap
